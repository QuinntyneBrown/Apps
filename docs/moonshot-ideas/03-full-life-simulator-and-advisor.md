# Full Life Simulator and Advisor

An app that builds a high-fidelity simulation of your entire life trajectory — career, health, relationships, finances, happiness — and lets you test major decisions before making them in reality.

## Why It Doesn't Work Today

Simulating a human life requires modeling the full complexity of society, economics, human relationships, health, and individual psychology. Current models can make narrow predictions but cannot integrate the thousands of interacting variables that determine life outcomes, nor can they model the emergent, chaotic dynamics of real human existence with any fidelity.

---

## Moonshot Ideas

### 1. Career Path Oracle

Input your skills, personality, values, financial situation, and risk tolerance. The app simulates 10,000 career trajectories for each option you're considering — not just salary projections but modeling job satisfaction, skill growth, relationship impact, burnout probability, industry disruption risk, and geographic implications. It shows you the full distribution of outcomes, not just the expected value.

### 2. Relationship Compatibility Deep Simulator

Two people input their communication styles, attachment patterns, life goals, conflict resolution tendencies, love languages, family expectations, and growth trajectories. The app simulates the relationship forward through major life events (career changes, kids, health crises, aging parents) and identifies the specific failure modes and resilience points — plus concrete interventions that would strengthen the relationship at each stage.

### 3. Financial Life Plan Stress Tester

Your complete financial picture is input along with your goals. The app simulates your financial life across thousands of economic scenarios — recessions, inflation spikes, job losses, medical emergencies, market crashes, windfalls. It identifies the exact sequence of decisions (savings rates, investment allocations, insurance purchases, career moves) that maximizes your probability of achieving your goals across the widest range of futures.

### 4. Health Trajectory Modeler

Your genetics, current biomarkers, lifestyle habits, environmental exposures, and medical history are input. The app models your biological aging across decades under different lifestyle interventions — exercise regimens, dietary changes, sleep optimization, stress management, preventive screenings. It shows you which interventions have the highest impact on your specific biology and when to start them.

### 5. Parenting Strategy Simulator

Expecting parents input their values, parenting philosophies, work constraints, support network, and the child's early temperament data. The app simulates childhood development under different parenting approaches — schooling choices, screen time policies, extracurricular strategies, discipline methods — and models outcomes for the child's cognitive development, emotional regulation, social skills, and long-term wellbeing.

### 6. City and Neighborhood Move Advisor

Describe your priorities (career, cost of living, community, schools, climate, healthcare, culture). The app doesn't just compare stats — it simulates your daily life in each candidate location: commute stress, social connection probability, career network density, exposure to natural disasters, political stability trajectory, and how each factor compounds over 5, 10, and 20 years.

### 7. Retirement Lifestyle Designer

Input your projected finances, health trajectory, social network, hobbies, and purpose-seeking tendencies. The app simulates different retirement lifestyles (full retirement, phased, encore career, relocation, volunteering) and models their impact on longevity, cognitive decline, depression risk, relationship quality, and financial sustainability — finding the lifestyle that maximizes your specific definition of a good late life.

### 8. Education Path Optimizer

A student inputs their interests, cognitive strengths, learning style, career aspirations, and financial constraints. The app simulates trajectories through different educational paths — specific universities, majors, gap years, bootcamps, apprenticeships, self-study — modeling not just earnings but intellectual fulfillment, network effects, identity formation, and adaptability to future economic shifts.

### 9. Grief and Loss Preparedness Planner

Not morbid — practical and compassionate. The app models the likely losses you'll experience over your lifetime (aging parents, potential health diagnoses, career disruptions, relationship changes) and helps you build emotional resilience, practical preparations, and support structures in advance. It simulates your coping under different scenarios and identifies where you're most vulnerable.

### 10. Legacy and Impact Maximizer

Input your values, skills, resources, and the change you want to see in the world. The app simulates hundreds of paths for maximizing your positive impact — career pivots, philanthropic strategies, mentoring approaches, creative projects, political engagement. It models the ripple effects of each choice across generations and identifies the highest-leverage actions available to you specifically.
