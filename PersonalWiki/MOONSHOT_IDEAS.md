# Personal Wiki - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

Personal Wiki provides a personal knowledge repository for reference information and how-tos. It manages pages, categories, and links to help users build and navigate their own structured knowledge base.

## Moonshot Ideas

1. **Autonomous Knowledge Gardener** - Continuously reviews your wiki for outdated information, broken assumptions, and stale references, flagging pages that need updating based on changes in the world, your life, or your other wiki entries, keeping your knowledge base evergreen without manual audits.

2. **Ambient Knowledge Capture** - Monitors your browsing, reading, conversations, and work activities to detect when you encounter information worth preserving, automatically drafting wiki entries from the content you interact with and suggesting where they fit in your existing knowledge structure.

3. **Intelligent Cross-Linking Engine** - Discovers non-obvious connections between wiki pages that you would never think to link manually, revealing how your knowledge about home repair connects to your notes on project management, or how your cooking techniques relate to your chemistry notes.

4. **Conversational Knowledge Retrieval** - Instead of searching and browsing, lets you ask your wiki questions in natural language ("How did I fix that leaky faucet last year?" or "What was that restaurant trick for crispy potatoes?") and synthesizes answers from across multiple pages.

5. **Knowledge Gap Detector** - Analyzes the topics you frequently search for externally, the questions you ask others, and the areas where your wiki is thin, then identifies the highest-value knowledge gaps and suggests specific learning paths to fill them.

6. **Contextual How-To Personalizer** - Adapts generic how-to pages based on your specific tools, equipment, skill level, and past experiences, so your "how to change oil" page reflects your specific car, your garage setup, and the mistakes you made last time.

7. **Multi-Modal Knowledge Integration** - Accepts knowledge in any form (voice memos while your hands are dirty, photos of whiteboard diagrams, screenshots of error messages, scanned handwritten notes) and automatically transcribes, categorizes, and integrates them into the correct wiki pages.

8. **Expertise Level Auto-Layering** - Maintains multiple depth levels for each topic, showing you the quick-reference summary when you just need a reminder, the detailed walkthrough when you are rusty, and the expert-level nuances when you are ready to go deeper, all within the same page.

9. **Collaborative Knowledge Merging** - When you meet someone with complementary expertise, intelligently merges relevant portions of their knowledge base with yours, resolving conflicts, noting different perspectives, and enriching your wiki with external expertise while maintaining your organizational structure.

10. **Predictive Knowledge Preparation** - Looks at your upcoming calendar, projects, and life events to pre-load and surface the wiki pages you will most likely need in the coming days, ensuring the right knowledge is at your fingertips before you realize you need it.
