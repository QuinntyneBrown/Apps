# GolfScoreTracker

## What It Does Today

GolfScoreTracker lets golfers log scores, track handicaps, record stats for each round, and review their performance history across courses and over time.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintelligence-Enabled)

1. **Stroke-by-Stroke Swing Diagnosis** -- Analyze video of each swing captured by a phone on the cart and provide tour-level biomechanical feedback, identifying the root cause of a slice, hook, or distance loss in the kinetic chain, not just the symptom.

2. **Personalized Practice Plan Generator** -- Based on detailed round data, identify the exact weaknesses costing the most strokes (not generic advice but "your 80-120 yard approach shots fade right 73% of the time on uphill lies") and build a practice session plan that targets the highest-ROI improvements.

3. **Real-Time Course Strategy Engine** -- For each hole being played, compute the optimal strategy given the player's actual skill distribution (not PGA averages), current wind, pin position, ground firmness, and the player's tendencies under pressure, recommending club selection and target lines.

4. **Mental Game Coach** -- Detect performance patterns tied to psychological states (scoring worse after a bad hole, choking on short putts when the round is going well, playing conservatively when they should be aggressive) and provide in-round mental coaching cues at the right moments.

5. **Playing Partner Dynamics Analyzer** -- Model how the player's performance changes based on who they are playing with (competitive friends, business clients, strangers) and provide social-context-aware advice for managing energy, focus, and scoring in different group dynamics.

6. **Course Condition Intelligence** -- Integrate real-time and historical course condition data (green speed, rough height, moisture, aeration schedules, recent maintenance) into strategy recommendations, adjusting club and shot suggestions for conditions the player has not yet experienced that day.

7. **Equipment Optimization Engine** -- Correlate shot data across thousands of rounds to identify which clubs in the bag are underperforming, which loft gaps exist, and which specific equipment changes (shaft flex, ball type, putter style) would produce the biggest scoring improvement for this player's swing.

8. **Handicap Trajectory Forecaster** -- Project the player's handicap trajectory months ahead based on their practice frequency, improvement rate, and upcoming playing schedule, then identify the specific interventions that would accelerate the path to their target handicap.

9. **Injury-Aware Performance Adjustment** -- When the player is dealing with a physical limitation (back stiffness, wrist soreness, reduced flexibility), adjust all strategy and swing recommendations to compensate, protecting the body while minimizing the scoring penalty.

10. **Tournament Preparation Architect** -- Before a competitive round, build a complete preparation plan for the specific course including practice round strategy, hole-by-hole game plans, scoring targets by section, and contingency strategies for trouble situations, all tailored to this player's strengths and weaknesses on this specific layout.
