# BucketListManager - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

BucketListManager allows users to create and manage a list of experiences, goals, and achievements they want to accomplish in their lifetime, tracking progress and completion status.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI-Powered)

1. **Feasibility Reality Checker** - For each bucket list item, provide a brutally honest assessment of what it actually takes (cost, physical fitness, time, prerequisites, legal requirements) and build a concrete, step-by-step path from where you are today to achieving it.

2. **Mortality-Aware Priority Optimizer** - Based on your age, health profile, and the physical or temporal constraints of each goal (you cannot summit Everest at 80, but you can write a novel), create an optimally ordered timeline that maximizes the number of goals you can realistically achieve.

3. **Serendipity and Opportunity Detector** - Monitor real-world events, travel deals, local happenings, and life circumstances to alert you when a window of opportunity opens for a bucket list item you had not planned to tackle yet (a rare eclipse near a planned vacation, a friend moving to a city you wanted to visit).

4. **Hidden Dream Excavator** - Analyze your past experiences, recurring interests, conversations, and media consumption to surface bucket list items you have never articulated but would deeply value, revealing aspirations you did not know you had.

5. **Experience Design Amplifier** - Take a simple bucket list item like "visit Japan" and design a deeply personalized version of that experience based on your specific interests, transforming generic goals into once-in-a-lifetime curated adventures.

6. **Shared Dream Matchmaker** - Identify bucket list overlaps among your friends, family, and contacts, enabling group experiences that are more meaningful and logistically easier than solo attempts.

7. **Post-Achievement Reflection Guide** - After completing a bucket list item, facilitate deep reflection on whether the experience met expectations, what you learned about yourself, and how it should reshape your remaining goals.

8. **Substitute Experience Finder** - When a bucket list item becomes permanently impossible (a destination is destroyed, a physical limitation develops), find alternative experiences that fulfill the same underlying desire in a different way.

9. **Life Chapter Milestone Integrator** - Connect bucket list items to major life transitions (retirement, empty nest, career change) and design experiences that give meaning and structure to these transitions rather than leaving them as vague "someday" goals.

10. **Legacy Experience Planner** - Identify which bucket list experiences would be most meaningful to share with specific people (teaching your child to surf, taking your parent to their homeland) and prioritize these intergenerational moments while they are still possible.
