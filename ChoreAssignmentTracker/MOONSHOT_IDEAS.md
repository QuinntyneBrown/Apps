# ChoreAssignmentTracker - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

ChoreAssignmentTracker helps households assign, schedule, and track completion of household chores among family members or roommates.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI-Powered)

1. **Fairness Perception Equalizer** - Go beyond equal time distribution to account for the cognitive load, unpleasantness, flexibility, and invisible labor (planning, noticing, managing) of each chore, ensuring that the distribution feels fair to every household member, not just equal on paper.

2. **Household Friction Detector and Resolver** - Identify emerging resentment patterns from chore completion data (one person always redoing another's work, passive-aggressive delays, quality disputes) and intervene with specific, constructive suggestions before conflicts escalate.

3. **Developmental Chore Curriculum for Children** - Design age-appropriate chore progressions for children that genuinely build life skills, responsibility, and confidence, adapting difficulty and expectations as they grow rather than using a static list.

4. **Energy and Mood Adaptive Scheduler** - Redistribute chores in real time based on each person's current state: shift demanding tasks away from someone who had a brutal work day, batch quick wins for someone who needs a sense of accomplishment, and protect recovery time after illness.

5. **Invisible Chore Surfacer** - Identify and quantify the household tasks that never appear on any chore list (remembering to buy birthday cards, scheduling vet appointments, noticing the soap is running low, managing the family calendar) and distribute this mental load equitably.

6. **Chore Efficiency Coach** - Analyze how each household member performs their chores and offer personalized technique improvements, optimal ordering, and time-saving approaches that could cut total household chore time significantly.

7. **Seasonal and Life-Event Chore Adaptor** - Automatically adjust the chore system for predictable seasonal changes (fall leaf cleanup, spring deep cleaning) and life events (new baby, exam period, holiday hosting) without requiring manual renegotiation.

8. **Motivation and Reward System Designer** - Understand what motivates each household member individually (gamification for the teenager, quiet appreciation for the introvert, visible progress for the data-lover) and tailor incentive structures accordingly.

9. **Home Maintenance Integration Planner** - Connect routine chores to deeper home maintenance needs, recognizing when regular cleaning reveals a maintenance issue (mold behind the bathroom mirror, pest evidence during sweeping) and escalating appropriately.

10. **Guest and Event Preparation Orchestrator** - When hosting is approaching, automatically generate a comprehensive preparation plan that distributes pre-event chores across household members with appropriate deadlines, accounting for each person's schedule and skills.
