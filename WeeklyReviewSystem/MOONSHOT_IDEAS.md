# WeeklyReviewSystem - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

WeeklyReviewSystem provides a structured framework for weekly reviews, helping users reflect on the past week's accomplishments, review goals, plan the upcoming week, track review streaks, and surface insights and trends from ongoing review data.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintelligence-Enabled)

1. **Automated Week-in-Review Draft Generator** - Compile data from your calendar, task manager, email, communication tools, and activity logs to pre-populate a comprehensive review of what actually happened this week, so you can focus on reflection instead of reconstruction.

2. **Pattern Recognition Across Months of Reviews** - Analyze your review history over months and years to surface recurring themes you might not notice: the same goal stalling every quarter, energy dipping every third week, or certain types of weeks consistently producing your best outcomes.

3. **Goal Progress Reality Checker** - Compare your stated weekly goals against actual outcomes across many weeks to identify which types of goals you consistently achieve and which you consistently set but never complete, then adjust goal-setting recommendations accordingly.

4. **Contextual Reflection Prompt Generator** - Instead of generic review questions, generate prompts that are specific to what happened in your week: "You had three back-to-back meeting days, how did that affect your deep work?" or "You shipped the feature two days early, what enabled that?"

5. **Cross-Domain Life Integration Analyzer** - Connect insights across work, health, relationships, finances, and personal growth to reveal how they interact: showing that your most productive work weeks correlate with consistent exercise, or that social isolation precedes your least satisfying reviews.

6. **Forward-Looking Week Optimizer** - Based on your review of this week and your upcoming calendar, suggest specific adjustments for next week: block time for the task you kept postponing, schedule recovery after the heavy meeting day, or pre-commit to the habit you noted wanting to build.

7. **Accountability Partner Intelligence** - If you share reviews with a partner or coach, surface the most important items for discussion, track which advice you actually followed, and identify patterns in what kind of external accountability is most effective for you.

8. **Emotional Trajectory Tracker** - Analyze the sentiment and tone of your review responses over time to detect gradual shifts in satisfaction, engagement, and well-being that might not be visible in any single weekly snapshot but become clear across months.

9. **Decision Quality Retroactive Analyzer** - When you note a decision during a review, flag it for future follow-up, then in subsequent reviews surface the outcome and help you build a track record of which types of decisions you make well and which types tend to go wrong.

10. **Annual Review and Life Strategy Synthesizer** - At year end, synthesize 52 weekly reviews into a comprehensive annual narrative: major themes, turning points, goals achieved and abandoned, relationships that grew or faded, and data-driven strategic recommendations for the year ahead.
