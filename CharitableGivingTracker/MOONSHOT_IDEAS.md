# CharitableGivingTracker - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

CharitableGivingTracker helps users log and organize their charitable donations, track giving history, and maintain records for tax deduction purposes.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI-Powered)

1. **Impact Per Dollar Maximizer** - Analyze every charity you have given to or are considering, using financial disclosures, program outcomes, independent evaluations, and on-the-ground reporting to estimate the concrete impact of each dollar and redirect your giving to where it does the most measurable good.

2. **Cause Area Gap Finder** - Identify underfunded cause areas that align with your values but are overlooked by most donors, showing you where your marginal dollar has outsized impact compared to popular, already well-funded organizations.

3. **Tax Strategy Optimizer** - Integrate with your full financial picture to optimize the timing, vehicle (direct gift, donor-advised fund, appreciated stock, QCD from IRA), and structure of your giving to maximize both charitable impact and tax benefit across multiple years.

4. **Charity Integrity Monitor** - Continuously monitor organizations you support for red flags: leadership scandals, financial mismanagement, mission drift, declining program effectiveness, or controversy, alerting you before problems become public.

5. **Giving Portfolio Diversification Advisor** - Treat your charitable giving like an investment portfolio, analyzing your allocation across cause areas, geographies, time horizons, and risk levels to build a balanced giving strategy that reflects your complete set of values.

6. **Counterfactual Impact Estimator** - For each donation, estimate what would have happened without your contribution: would someone else have funded it, would the program have continued at reduced scale, or was your gift truly the difference between the program existing or not.

7. **Personal Values to Cause Mapper** - Through deep conversation about your life experiences, moral intuitions, and priorities, map your values onto specific cause areas and organizations you may not have discovered on your own, revealing alignment you would not have found through browsing charity websites.

8. **Recurring Giving Rebalancer** - Automatically review your recurring donations against updated effectiveness data and your evolving financial situation, recommending rebalancing just like a financial advisor would with investments.

9. **Volunteer Time and Skills Impact Calculator** - Quantify the impact of your non-monetary contributions (volunteering, board service, pro bono work) alongside cash donations to give a complete picture of your charitable footprint and identify where your time versus money has more impact.

10. **Legacy and Estate Giving Planner** - Model different scenarios for charitable estate planning (bequests, charitable remainder trusts, foundation creation) to show how your giving can continue and grow beyond your lifetime, aligned with your long-term vision.
