# VideoGameCollectionManager - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

VideoGameCollectionManager helps users catalog their video game collection, track owned titles across platforms, manage wishlists, and organize their library with details about each game including platform, genre, and completion status.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintelligence-Enabled)

1. **Personalized Game Recommendation Engine** - Analyze your play history, completion patterns, genre preferences, session length tendencies, and which games you abandoned versus loved to recommend games from your backlog or new releases with high predicted enjoyment.

2. **Collection Value Tracker with Market Intelligence** - Monitor real-time market values for physical games, track price trends for retro and collector titles, predict which games in your collection will appreciate, and alert you to sell-high opportunities.

3. **Backlog Priority Optimizer** - Analyze your backlog against your available gaming time, game length estimates, and personal taste to generate an optimized play order that maximizes enjoyment per hour and avoids genre fatigue.

4. **Cross-Platform Duplicate and Overlap Detector** - Identify games you own on multiple platforms, games included in subscription services you already pay for, and games in bundles you purchased but never redeemed, eliminating redundant purchases.

5. **Completion Path Advisor** - For games you are currently playing, provide spoiler-free guidance on how to approach completion, estimate remaining time, identify missable content, and suggest when to move on if a game does not match your preferences.

6. **Gaming Session Context Matcher** - Based on your current mood, available time, energy level, and whether you are playing solo or with others, recommend the perfect game from your collection for right now, not just in general.

7. **Price History and Deal Prediction** - Track historical pricing across all storefronts for games on your wishlist, predict when the next sale is likely based on historical patterns, and recommend whether to buy now or wait.

8. **Collection Showcase and Trading Intelligence** - Generate a shareable collection showcase, identify collectors looking for games you have duplicates of, suggest trades that benefit both parties, and estimate fair trade values for physical games.

9. **Hardware Compatibility and Preservation Advisor** - Track which hardware you need to play each game in your collection, alert you when hardware becomes hard to find, recommend emulation solutions, and identify preservation risks for your physical media.

10. **Gaming Habit and Well-Being Monitor** - Track your gaming patterns to identify unhealthy trends (excessive session lengths, sleep impact, spending spikes during sales) and provide gentle, non-judgmental insights that help maintain gaming as a positive hobby.
