# FriendGroupEventCoordinator

## What It Does Today

FriendGroupEventCoordinator helps groups of friends plan and organize social events, managing availability, preferences, RSVPs, and logistics so that getting together is less of a scheduling headache.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintelligence-Enabled)

1. **Social Graph Health Monitor** -- Track the strength and frequency of connections within the friend group over time, detecting when relationships are fading or subgroups are forming, and proactively suggest events that reconnect drifting members before the distance becomes permanent.

2. **Personality-Aware Event Design** -- Understand each friend's social energy, introversion/extroversion spectrum, current life stress, and activity preferences deeply enough to design events where everyone is genuinely comfortable, not just events where most people tolerate the plan.

3. **Implicit Availability Detection** -- Go beyond explicit calendar availability to understand when people are actually free and willing (someone who is technically free Friday night but just worked 60 hours does not want a high-energy outing) and find windows where the group's real availability overlaps.

4. **Conflict-Sensitive Planning** -- Detect interpersonal tensions, recent arguments, or awkward relationship dynamics within the group and design seating, activity groupings, and invitation lists that avoid uncomfortable situations without making the mediation visible.

5. **Life-Stage Bridge Builder** -- As friend groups diverge in life stages (some have kids, some are single, some moved away), design hybrid events that genuinely work across these differences rather than defaulting to the lowest common denominator.

6. **Spontaneous Gathering Catalyst** -- Detect real-time conditions where an impromptu hangout would work (three friends are all within a mile of each other on a free evening, the weather just cleared up, a friend is having a rough day) and spark the gathering at the perfect moment.

7. **Shared Experience Optimizer** -- For each proposed event, predict the specific moments that will generate shared stories and inside jokes (the bonding currency of friendships) and steer toward activities with the highest potential for memorable shared experiences.

8. **Budget Sensitivity Harmonizer** -- Understand each friend's financial situation without anyone having to disclose it explicitly, and suggest activities and cost-splitting approaches that never put anyone in the uncomfortable position of being priced out or subsidized visibly.

9. **Group Tradition Generator** -- Identify emerging patterns in what the group enjoys and propose formalizing them into traditions (annual camping trip, monthly game night, birthday rituals) with logistics handled automatically, giving the group a sense of identity and continuity.

10. **Reunion Architect** -- For friend groups scattered across cities or countries, plan complex reunion events that optimize travel costs, find geographically fair meeting points, account for visa requirements and time zones, and structure multi-day agendas that make the rare time together count.
