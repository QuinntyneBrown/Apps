# FocusSessionTracker

## What It Does Today

FocusSessionTracker helps users plan, time, and review focused work sessions, tracking how much deep work they accomplish and identifying patterns in their productivity.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintelligence-Enabled)

1. **Cognitive State Detection** -- Infer the user's real-time mental state (flow, distraction, fatigue, creative block) from typing patterns, application switching behavior, and work output quality, then adapt session length and break timing to match what the brain actually needs right now.

2. **Optimal Work Scheduling** -- Learn each user's unique ultradian rhythms, energy cycles, and cognitive peaks across days, weeks, and seasons, then auto-schedule the right type of work (creative, analytical, administrative) into the windows where that specific person does that specific type of work best.

3. **Distraction Root Cause Analysis** -- Go beyond tracking that a distraction happened to understand why it happened (anxiety about an unresolved email, blood sugar drop, boredom from a task mismatch) and address the root cause rather than just blocking the symptom.

4. **Task Decomposition for Flow** -- Analyze an upcoming task and automatically break it into sub-tasks sized and sequenced to maximize the probability of entering and sustaining a flow state, based on the user's demonstrated flow triggers and patterns.

5. **Environmental Optimization** -- Correlate focus session quality with environmental variables (noise levels, lighting, temperature, time of day, what was eaten, how much sleep was logged) and generate actionable recommendations for the ideal working conditions for this specific person.

6. **Inter-Session Momentum Preservation** -- When a focus session ends mid-thought, capture enough context about the mental state, open questions, and reasoning threads that the next session can resume with near-zero ramp-up time, even days later.

7. **Collaborative Focus Coordination** -- For teams, find windows where multiple people can simultaneously enter deep work on shared problems, protecting those windows from meetings and interruptions while ensuring the team stays synchronized.

8. **Burnout Early Warning System** -- Detect the subtle precursors of burnout (declining session quality despite maintained duration, increasing reliance on stimulants, narrowing of creative range) weeks before the user would notice, and prescribe specific recovery protocols.

9. **Skill Acquisition Acceleration** -- When the user is learning something new, optimize session structure for the specific type of skill being acquired (spaced repetition for memorization, interleaved practice for problem-solving, deliberate practice cycles for motor skills) and adapt as mastery develops.

10. **Life-Work Integration Optimizer** -- Understand the user's full life context (not just work) and find focus session schedules that maximize both productivity and life satisfaction, recognizing that the best work often comes from people whose non-work needs are genuinely met.
