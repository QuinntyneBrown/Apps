# CouplesGoalTracker - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

CouplesGoalTracker helps romantic partners set, track, and work toward shared goals together, from financial targets to lifestyle aspirations and relationship milestones.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI-Powered)

1. **Implicit Goal Conflict Detector** - Identify when partners' individual goals silently conflict with shared goals (one partner's career ambition undermining the shared goal of more family time) and surface these tensions constructively before they become resentments.

2. **Contribution Equity Analyzer** - Track and visualize each partner's contribution to shared goals in a way that accounts for different contribution types (financial, emotional, logistical, creative), preventing the common trap where one type of contribution is valued more than others.

3. **Relationship Health Correlator** - Detect patterns between goal progress and relationship satisfaction, identifying which shared activities strengthen the relationship and which create stress, so couples can adjust their goals to support rather than strain their partnership.

4. **Communication Style Mediator** - Understand each partner's communication style around goals (one is a planner, the other is spontaneous; one needs verbal processing, the other needs written reflection) and facilitate goal-setting conversations in a format that works for both.

5. **Life Stage Transition Planner** - Anticipate and prepare for major transitions (moving in together, marriage, first child, career change, retirement) by proactively suggesting goals and conversations that couples typically wish they had addressed earlier.

6. **Financial Goal Fairness Modeler** - For couples with income disparities, model different approaches to shared financial goals (proportional contribution, equal contribution, needs-based) and help partners find an arrangement that both perceive as fair.

7. **Intimacy and Connection Goal Facilitator** - Go beyond logistical goals to help couples set and track relationship quality goals (weekly date nights, daily check-ins, physical affection, shared hobbies) with the sensitivity and nuance these personal topics require.

8. **Extended Family and Social Goal Negotiator** - Help couples navigate goals that involve extended family (holiday traditions, caregiving responsibilities, family business obligations) where external pressures and loyalties create complexity.

9. **Dream Divergence Reconciler** - When partners discover their long-term visions diverge significantly (one wants to move abroad, the other wants to stay near family), facilitate a structured exploration of compromise, creative solutions, and the real priorities underlying each position.

10. **Celebration and Milestone Recognizer** - Actively track progress toward goals and prompt meaningful celebrations at milestones, understanding that couples often fail to acknowledge their shared achievements, which undermines motivation and the sense of being a team.
