# FamilyCalendarEventPlanner

## What It Does Today

FamilyCalendarEventPlanner helps families create, schedule, and manage shared events and appointments across household members, keeping everyone on the same page with a unified calendar view.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintelligence-Enabled)

1. **Predictive Conflict Resolution** -- Automatically detect when a newly proposed event will cause a downstream cascade of problems (e.g., scheduling soccer practice the same week as finals) and propose a restructured week that optimizes for every family member's wellbeing and obligations.

2. **Whole-Family Energy Modeling** -- Model each family member's physical and emotional energy levels across the week using behavioral signals, then schedule high-demand events (dentist visits, recitals, difficult conversations) at times when the relevant people are at peak capacity.

3. **Life-Stage Aware Long-Range Planning** -- Automatically project calendar needs months or years ahead based on children's ages, school transitions, aging parents' health trajectories, and career milestones, generating a living roadmap the family can refine together.

4. **Cross-Household Diplomatic Scheduling** -- For divorced or blended families, negotiate custody schedules, holiday splits, and shared events across multiple households with competing priorities, generating proposals that are fair and legally compliant.

5. **Invisible Labor Equalization** -- Track who is doing the unseen coordination work (driving, meal prep, appointment booking) and flag when the burden is lopsided, suggesting concrete redistributions that account for each person's real availability and skills.

6. **Cultural and Religious Calendar Fusion** -- For multicultural families, intelligently weave together observances, holidays, and traditions from multiple cultural backgrounds, resolving conflicts and suggesting meaningful ways to honor overlapping celebrations.

7. **Real-Time Day Reconstruction** -- When a key event falls through (flight canceled, school closed, someone gets sick), instantly regenerate the rest of the day or week for all affected family members, coordinating backup childcare, rescheduled meetings, and meal plans.

8. **Child Development-Aligned Scheduling** -- Analyze each child's developmental stage and recommend an ideal balance of structured activities, free play, social time, and rest, flagging when the calendar is over- or under-scheduled for their age.

9. **Extended Family Relationship Maintenance** -- Track how long it has been since the family connected with grandparents, cousins, or close friends, and proactively find windows for calls, visits, or shared activities that fit everyone's calendars across time zones.

10. **Family Goal Integration Engine** -- Connect calendar scheduling to the family's stated goals (save money, get healthier, spend more time together) and score each week's schedule against those goals, suggesting swaps that move the needle without adding stress.
