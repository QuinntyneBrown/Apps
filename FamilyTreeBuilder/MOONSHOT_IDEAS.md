# FamilyTreeBuilder

## What It Does Today

FamilyTreeBuilder lets users construct and visualize their family genealogy, adding relatives, dates, and relationships to build a structured tree that can be shared with family members.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintelligence-Enabled)

1. **Autonomous Record Discovery** -- Continuously scour digitized historical records, immigration databases, census archives, church registries, and newspaper archives worldwide to find and verify new ancestors without any manual searching, presenting findings with confidence scores and source citations.

2. **DNA-to-Tree Integration** -- Ingest raw DNA test results from multiple family members and third-party databases, then resolve the genetic data into precise relationship placements on the tree, identifying unknown parentage, adoption origins, and correcting misattributed lineages.

3. **Historical Context Narrator** -- For each ancestor, generate a rich biographical narrative explaining what their daily life was likely like based on their occupation, location, era, and socioeconomic status, turning bare names and dates into vivid human stories.

4. **Cross-Family Tree Merging** -- Detect when two separately maintained family trees share common ancestors, then intelligently merge them while resolving conflicting data, duplicate entries, and naming variations across languages and transliteration systems.

5. **Migration Path Reconstruction** -- Trace the geographic journey of a family line across centuries, mapping migrations triggered by wars, famines, economic opportunities, or persecution, and visualizing the path on an interactive historical map.

6. **Hereditary Health Risk Mapping** -- Analyze causes of death, known medical conditions, and longevity patterns across the tree to surface potential hereditary health risks and suggest which living family members might benefit from specific screenings.

7. **Lost Living Relative Finder** -- Identify branches of the family that lost contact (due to emigration, war, family disputes) and locate likely living descendants using public records, social media presence analysis, and genealogical databases, facilitating reunions.

8. **Language and Cultural Heritage Recovery** -- Determine what languages, dialects, religious practices, and cultural traditions ancestors carried, tracking how these were preserved, blended, or lost across generations, and offering resources to reconnect with fading heritage.

9. **Oral History Interpreter** -- Process recorded oral histories from elderly relatives, extracting names, dates, places, and relationships from rambling or fragmented stories, then cross-reference these against the existing tree to fill in gaps and correct errors.

10. **Probabilistic Ancestor Modeling** -- For brick-wall ancestors where records simply do not exist, generate probabilistic models of who they likely were based on the demographic, geographic, and genetic constraints of their known descendants, providing the best available picture of the unknown.
