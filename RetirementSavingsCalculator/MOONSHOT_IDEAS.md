# RetirementSavingsCalculator - Moonshot Ideas

## What It Does Today

RetirementSavingsCalculator helps users plan for retirement by modeling savings scenarios, projecting growth based on contribution rates and investment returns, and comparing different retirement timelines. Users can manage multiple scenarios with varying assumptions.

## Moonshot Ideas (AGI/Superintenence-Enabled)

1. **Hyper-Personalized Longevity Modeling** - Integrate health records, genetics, lifestyle data, and actuarial science to predict your individual life expectancy with high precision, then dynamically adjust retirement savings targets and drawdown strategies accordingly.

2. **Real-Time Tax-Optimized Contribution Routing** - Continuously analyze changing tax laws, your income trajectory, and account balances to recommend the exact dollar amount to put in each account type (401k, Roth IRA, HSA, taxable) every pay period for maximum after-tax retirement wealth.

3. **Macro-Economic Scenario Stress Testing** - Simulate thousands of future economic scenarios including stagflation, currency crises, healthcare cost explosions, and social security reform, then show the probability distribution of retirement outcomes and recommend hedging strategies.

4. **Spending Pattern Retirement Translator** - Analyze your actual spending history and project how each category will change in retirement (healthcare up, commuting down, travel up initially then down), producing a far more accurate retirement budget than simple percentage-of-income rules.

5. **Autonomous Portfolio Rebalancing Advisor** - Monitor your retirement accounts across all providers and recommend rebalancing moves that account for tax lot optimization, fund overlap, expense ratios, and your evolving risk capacity as retirement approaches.

6. **Social Security Claiming Strategy Optimizer** - Model every possible Social Security claiming strategy for you and your spouse, accounting for health, income needs, survivor benefits, tax implications, and opportunity costs to identify the strategy that maximizes lifetime benefits.

7. **Healthcare Cost Crystal Ball** - Project your specific future healthcare costs based on your health trajectory, insurance market trends, Medicare policy changes, and geographic cost variation, then integrate these into your retirement plan as a first-class expense category.

8. **Life Event Impact Simulator** - Instantly model how major life events (divorce, inheritance, disability, caring for aging parents, a child's college expenses) would alter your retirement timeline, and pre-compute contingency plans for each.

9. **Inflation-Adjusted Lifestyle Preservation Engine** - Go beyond simple inflation adjustments to model how your specific lifestyle costs will evolve, including housing market trends in your target retirement location, hobby cost trajectories, and insurance premium growth.

10. **Retirement Readiness Negotiation Coach** - When you are considering a job change, analyze the total retirement impact of each offer (salary, 401k match, pension, stock options, healthcare) and generate a counter-offer strategy that maximizes long-term retirement outcomes over short-term salary.
